# Package attribution

Open `CREDITS.txt` for the consolidated project, catalogue, imagery, and
software credits. It is also available through Settings > Open Credits.
Keep the host `LICENSE` and `PRIVACY.md` with `licenses/Universe-LICENSE.txt`, `licenses/Universe-NOTICE.md`,
`licenses/Three-LICENSE.txt`, `licenses/OpenTK-LICENSE.txt`,
`licenses/WebView2-LICENSE.txt`, `licenses/WebView2-NOTICE.txt`, and
`licenses/DotNet-ThirdPartyNotices.txt` with the package.

The bundled Reach map records and source links are in
`dist/textures/reach-assets.manifest.json`. Optional enhanced images are not
part of the base package. Their records are maintained in the host source
repository at `VisualAssets/reach-assets.manifest.json` and
`VisualAssets/REACH_IMAGERY_V1.md`. Do not describe optional images with
unresolved redistribution terms as cleared for public redistribution. The
public loader excludes both Ceres and both Mercury records from its download offer.
