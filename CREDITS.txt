The Reach Screensaver - Credits

The Reach Screensaver uses a production build of Your Eyes In Space, originally
created by Berk Comba. The original repository is
https://github.com/berkcomba/youreyesinspace.

The screensaver host repository is
https://github.com/NoReturns/TheReachScreensaver.

Your Eyes In Space source code is MIT licensed. Its HYG-derived star catalogue
comes from the HYG Database by David Nash (Astronexus) and uses CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

Planetary data and images include NASA/JPL orbital and spacecraft data, NASA
Blue Marble imagery, NASA/USGS Viking Mars imagery, NASA/JPL/Space Science
Institute Cassini Jupiter imagery, and NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied
Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute New Horizons Pluto imagery.
The complete package includes further source and license details in
ATTRIBUTION.md, licenses/Universe-NOTICE.md, and the asset manifests.

The native host includes OpenTK 4.9.4 (MIT; Stefanos Apostolopoulos and the
Open Toolkit contributors), StbImageSharp 2.30.15 (public domain), the
Microsoft WebView2 SDK loader, and .NET runtime components. Their detailed
notices are in the complete package's licenses folder. The WebView2 Evergreen
Runtime is a separate prerequisite and is not redistributed here.
