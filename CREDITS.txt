Bundled Reach planetary maps are credited individually in
dist/textures/reach-assets.manifest.json. They include NASA Earth Observatory
Blue Marble Next Generation; NASA SVS, Ernie Wright, LROC/Arizona State
University and LOLA Moon data; NASA/JPL-Caltech/USGS Viking Mars and moon
maps; NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute Cassini Jupiter imagery; and
NASA/JPL-Caltech/USGS Voyager and Galileo moon maps. The native host also
embeds Earth Blue Marble, Viking Mars, Cassini Jupiter, and New Horizons
Pluto maps; their source products are recorded in the host repository's
Assets/Planets/SOURCES.md. NASA, JPL, and USGS source credits do not imply
endorsement of The Reach Screensaver.
Native map source pages: Earth https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble
Mars https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/
Jupiter https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA07782
Pluto https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA11707

Optional enhanced maps are not included in the base package. Their individual
source teams, image processing, known coverage limits, and pinned file hashes
are recorded in the host repository's VisualAssets/REACH_IMAGERY_V1.md and
VisualAssets/reach-assets.manifest.json. Redistribution terms for the DLR
Ceres mosaic remain unverified. The exact enhanced Mercury JPEG source and
derivation also remain unverified. Both Ceres and both Mercury records are
excluded from the public download offer; bundled or procedural fallback stays
available. Existing local files are not deleted.
Public download availability alone does not establish redistribution rights.

Six additional Phobos, Deimos, and Mimas maps are hosted on the imagery Release
but are not offered by the v1.0 app. The Phobos and Deimos grayscale maps are
derived from Philip Stooke's Stooke Small Bodies Maps V3.0. Required citation:
Stooke, P., Stooke Small Bodies Maps V3.0,
MULTI-SA-MULTI-6-STOOKEMAPS-V3.0, NASA Planetary Data System, 2015.
The PDS source guide states that the maps are public domain with proper credit
and are for visualization, not photometric analysis:
https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds3/multi_mission/MULTI_SA_MULTI_6_STOOKEMAPS_V3_0/document/00_map_guide.html
The Mimas grayscale map derives from Cassini Global Map - June 2017,
PIA17214. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute.
https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/mimas-global-map-june-2017/
Source files, export details, and image limitations are recorded in
VisualAssets/REACH_IMAGERY_V1.md. These credits do not imply endorsement.
