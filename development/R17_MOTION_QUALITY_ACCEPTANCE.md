# R17 motion quality acceptance - isolated developer experiment

Date: 2026-10-28

Decision: **NO-GO for the 2433x2007 cap as a quality candidate in this three-monitor fixture.** The cap saves dedicated GPU memory reproducibly and retains the sampled black-hole silhouette and planet join, but the slow-moving deep-space stars become visibly larger and brighter. This is a developer technical decision, not owner visual acceptance. B1 remains NO-GO. No production renderer, shader, lens, display configuration, installed screensaver, or journey data was changed.

## Configuration and method

- Hardware: owner's RTX 4080 and existing 8320x2160 virtual desktop. Physical displays, normalized to virtual origin: left 3840x2160 at (0,0), primary 2560x1440 at (3840,720), right 1920x1080 at (6400,1080). Windows layout was read, not changed.
- Payloads: R16 `unbounded` 3244x2675 and `cap2433` 2433x2007. Both use 2x sector MSAA, the same eight sector identities (left 3, primary 3, right 2), cylindrical screen rays, perspective camera aspect and angular coverage, native bloom, 512-pixel guard, full physical output, lens mathematics, exposure and tone mapping. [R17 variant manifest](r17-variants/manifest.json), [aligned join manifest](r17-join-aligned-variants/manifest.json).
- Isolation: each case copied a developer-only bundle and developer host to a new `run-<GUID>` directory with its own WebView2 profile. No owner voyage or installed profile was opened. Runs were serial. The host inhibited system and display sleep during each 60-second motion interval. It stopped and closed the three windows after draining evidence.
- The R17 bundles have the exact R16 bundle bytes as a prefix. Only [developer rAF and readback code](r17_browser.js) was appended. The host change is confined to the existing developer entry point, `ReachDeveloperApplication.CaptureR17` and `Window.StartR17` / `DrainR17` in `Application/ReachDeveloperApplication.cs`. The fixture calls one `Engine.render()` per animation callback and reads final default-framebuffer pixels after selected renders. It adds no scene render pass. R16's direct static draw counts remain 160 for the planet/star fixture and 412 for the black-hole fixture, equal between target sizes; R17 did not recount draws per moving callback.
- Fixture state is deterministic: `galaxy-sector` for the peripheral planet, `galaxy-left-seam` for small stars, and `hole-26-left` for the 26-radii black hole. Material shader time stays 1234 and black-hole time stays 0, as in the native fixture state. Scene seeds, bodies, pose origin, roll, composition shift, bloom and tone mapping are fixed. Smooth yaw trajectories are: planet 23 to 39 degrees over 60 seconds; aligned planet join 23 to 47 degrees; stars -78 to -77.6 degrees; black hole `8 sin(2 pi t / 20000)` degrees for three cycles. The joined path was a supplemental coverage pass, not a renderer variant.
- Each page rendered continuously under `requestAnimationFrame` for 60 seconds. At integer virtual seconds 1 through 59, it rendered the mathematically exact yaw for that timestamp and read one native RGBA8 crop. Between captures the camera used continuous elapsed time, not integer-degree steps. Control and candidate runs have equal base fixture state keys, virtual milliseconds, yaw values, crop rectangles and fixed shader time for every compared frame. Actual callback timestamps differ; frames were paired by exact virtual state, not by wall-clock coincidence. [The frame index and raw absolute paths](r17-runs/R17_ANALYSIS.json) record every paired timestamp, yaw, source path and crop.
- The left and primary final pixels are stored as bottom-up native RGBA8 `.rgba` files. Review PNGs flip rows without resizing. MP4 files present matched crops side by side at 4 samples per playback second, compressing 59 seconds to about 15 seconds; use source RGBA or PNG for pixel judgments. Synchronous readback took a median of roughly 31-40 ms on the left display, once per second. Callback intervals from these runs include that overhead and are **not GPU frame times or ordinary uncaptured pacing**. R16's separate, uncaptured 30-second rAF measurements are the ordinary pacing reference.

NVIDIA adapter memory and GPU utilization are adapter-wide and cannot be assigned to these processes. R16's uncaptured moving-black-hole medians were 4418 versus 3222 MiB, a 1195 MiB saving. The R17 capture-run differences span 1146-1265 MiB and support the reproducibility of an approximately 1.2 GiB saving, without claiming a precise universal allocation delta. The adapter-wide readings vary more with other desktop activity and are corroboration only. First-to-last counter samples do not show a consistent within-run rise; see [analysis JSON](r17-runs/R17_ANALYSIS.json) for each run's first and last ten-second medians, adapter figures, utilization and callback interval distributions.

All ten reports show 59 left and 59 primary samples, no capture overflow, no reported WebGL readback error and no `webglcontextlost` event. Every initial status reports three 2x targets on left, three on primary and two on right at the intended dimension for its variant, with unchanged center angles. The right display rendered throughout but supplied no pixel crop. Its callbacks and status were recorded. The initial complete-display captures and the 1 Hz crops do not prove zero transient driver errors between sampled readbacks.

The deep-space fixture is sparse. Existing native fixture capabilities did not provide a representative dense starfield without changing scene content, so dense-field crowding and bloom interaction are **unavailable in R17**. The cap's sparse-star degradation is directly visible regardless. Star patch flux varies over the 59 slow-motion samples by about 0.9% coefficient of variation in control and 1.7% in candidate; this suggests greater sampled brightness variation, but 1 Hz sampling and a single tracked point do not establish a perceptual shimmer rate. There is no invented flicker threshold. Owner full-resolution motion review remains separate.

## Fourth-above geometry and limits

R16's hypothetical fourth-above desktop doubles virtual height to 4320 with no physical or simulated fourth rendering window in R17. At the same 2433x2007 cap, center source sampling density falls from approximately 0.75 to 0.375 sector texels per physical output pixel. To **preserve even the current candidate's 0.75 density**, the fourth-above model needs about **4866x4012 per sector** (seven sectors in that modeled layout). R16's source-derived 2x color/depth/MSAA estimate for those seven targets is about 4692 MiB before fourth-context, bloom, cube, browser and driver allocations. These are geometry/resource models, not fourth-monitor measurements. Since 2433 fails the present star-quality pilot, preserving its density is not itself an accepted quality floor. A fixed cap does not remove the virtual-height dependence of focal length, rays, UV density or tile-camera LOD, and it does not establish arbitrary-layout support.

## Decision and next validation

**NO-GO for 2433x2007 at 2x MSAA as the tested three-monitor developer quality candidate.** The memory saving is real enough to keep resolution research worthwhile, and the black-hole/join results do not reveal a gross geometry regression. Persistent broadening and brightening of small stars is a visible degradation in matched native frames during slow subpixel motion. No production promotion or B1 acceptance follows.

The smallest next quality experiment is a separately isolated intermediate sector size or source-sampling adjustment, compared with 3244 at exact-state slow-star motion and a denser deterministic star fixture. It should retain the same angular projection and be reviewed at native resolution by the owner before any acceptance claim. R17 did not make that change.

Validation performed: repository status inspected before work; `BUILD_FROM_SOURCE.md` and fixture/host source read; R17 bundle prefix hashes checked against R16; both R17 and aligned join bundles passed `node --check`; developer host published with `--no-restore`; ten isolated 60-second runs completed; all frame state/crop pairs verified by [analysis script](r17_analyze.py); raw captures, PNGs and synchronized MP4 sequences generated and inspected.
